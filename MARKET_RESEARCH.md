# Market Direction Notes — September 2026

This product direction was checked against current music/creative career platforms and industry resources.

Observed patterns:
- Niche music job boards remain valuable because generic boards create discovery noise.
- Indian creative platforms increasingly combine jobs/gigs with talent networks and portfolio discovery.
- Newer music-network products emphasize verified profiles, gigs, booking, safe payment and organization sourcing.
- Casting/creator marketplaces increasingly use trust primitives such as verified productions, explicit compensation and anti-pay-to-apply rules.
- Music-industry careers span operational functions such as rights, metadata, royalties, campaigns, touring and publishing—not only artists/labels.

Sources reviewed:
- Symphonic Distribution, “10 Music Industry Job Sites To Help You Land Your Next Role” (20 Apr 2026)
- Tarana Music — professional music network / gig marketplace (India)
- NETSA — gigs and creative talent marketplace (India)
- Talentrack — media & entertainment talent/recruiter platform (India)
- Suyaara — casting calls / creator campaigns with verified productions and stated compensation
- CultureCareers — Indian creative-industry jobs and talent network
- MusicCareers — global music-industry job site
- Musicians Rights UK — music-industry careers guide (reviewed May 2026)

The conclusion: Verse should compete on **music-native identity + opportunity context + trust + hiring workflow**, not on having a prettier list of jobs.
